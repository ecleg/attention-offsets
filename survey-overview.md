# Survey Overview: Sustainability Research (University Students)

## How to read this
- This is a descriptive summary of a student survey.
- Counts (`n`) vary by question because some respondents skipped items.
- “Multi-select” questions allow respondents to pick more than one option, so totals can exceed 100%.

## Key takeaways
- AI usage is common: daily + weekly usage is a majority among respondents who answered the frequency question.
- The top stated concern about AI is environmental impact (largest single category).
- Attention is concentrated on Instagram and YouTube—useful channels for “attention-funded offsets” positioning.
- A meaningful share reports using ad blockers on YouTube, which matters for ad-based funding assumptions.

## What this implies for “attention-funded offsets”
- Positioning: Environmental concern is the largest single stated worry about AI; messaging can connect “AI convenience” to “visible mitigation.”
- Channel fit: Instagram + YouTube are near-universal among respondents to Q9; these are natural places to test offset-funding messaging.
- Measurement caution: Ad-blocker usage suggests that revenue assumptions should include scenarios for reduced ad delivery.
